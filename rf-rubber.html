About RF Laminated Rubber Tile

COMMERCIAL GRADE — BUILT TO PERFORM

Our RF Laminated Rubber Tile is manufactured from high wear-resistant and environment-friendly elastic surface cushion and shock-absorbing foam composite. The reinforced laminated structure enhances stability while delivering the high elasticity and comfort required in commercial environments.

Used extensively in gyms, fitness centres, kindergartens, children's playgrounds, and any space requiring high safety factors. The tile format enables fast, low-cost installation and removal — significantly reducing construction and ongoing maintenance costs compared to poured rubber or vinyl alternatives.

Available in 8 standard colour codes (RF1501–RF1508) with professional multi-colour speckle patterns. Manufactured from recycled rubber materials as part of our Sustainability and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) commitment — Cradle-to-Cradle development in practice.
