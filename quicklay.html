Why Choose QuickLay?

SMART. DURABLE.
FAST TO INSTALL.

QuickLay Acrylic Mat — our prefabricated acrylic court surface — is engineered for the demands of modern pickleball and tennis courts. Built for performance, durability, and speed of installation, it is the smart alternative to traditional on-site liquid acrylic systems.

Traditional acrylic is a liquid epoxy material applied on-site — widely recognised for tennis and pickleball courts, especially at the US Open. It offers good anti-slip properties but is rigid and prone to cracking. QuickLay PAP flooring is prefabricated, not affected by base cracking, and installs in 2 hours versus 7–14 days for traditional acrylic, subject to weather conditions.

The one-piece colouring means seamless design for better durability and aesthetics — no fading, no oil bleeding, stable colour indoors and outdoors. The adjustable sand texture allows speed control tuned to athlete-specific performance requirements.

Certified by the ITF (International Tennis Federation). Available in thousands of Pantone and RAL custom colours with custom logos and patterns for branded club courts.

Construction

ENGINEERED
LAYER BY LAYER

4 distinct layers work together for professional-grade performance, durability, and consistent ball behaviour on every court.

Layer 1 · Top
Modified Acrylic + Sand Texture
AC surface treatment technology. Adjustable sand texture for athlete-specific speed control. Non-toxic, odorless, self-cleaning.
Layer 2
Wear-Resistant Outdoor Layer
High-density wear layer for outdoor UV exposure. Maintains anti-slip performance and colour stability over years of outdoor use.
Layer 3
Double-Layer Polyester Fibre Mesh
Dual polyester fibre mesh cloth reinforcement. Provides dimensional stability and prevents deformation across the full court.
Layer 4 · Base
High-Density Anti-Displacement Base
High-density base that grips the substrate without adhesives. Boosts ball response and court speed.
