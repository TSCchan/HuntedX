The World's Most Trusted Indoor Sports Surface

Taraflex® is the most widely specified sports surface in the world. For over seven decades, Gerflor has engineered flooring that protects athletes at every level — from school halls to Olympic arenas.

Selection for 10 consecutive Olympic Games is the ultimate proof of performance. Every surface must pass the scrutiny of the world's best athletes and international governing bodies — and Taraflex® has passed every time.

With a complete range of P1/P2/P3 shock absorption, Taraflex® offers the perfect solution for all users and all usage intensities — from leisure recreation to elite championship competition.

About Us

MALAYSIA'S SPORTS
FLOORING SPECIALIST

Rentak Hiasan Setia Sdn Bhd is the sole distributor of Taraflex® by Gerflor in Malaysia. Taraflex® is our flagship sports flooring brand, alongside RT SportTile, QuickLay, and RF Rubberlay.

Headquartered at Phileo Damansara, Petaling Jaya, we supply, install, and project-manage sports flooring for badminton courts, futsal pitches, pickleball courts, gyms, and multipurpose halls across Malaysia.

From Olympic-grade sport flooring to snap-together outdoor modular tiles, our four-brand portfolio covers every sport, every budget, and every surface — backed by full installation service and after-sales support.
