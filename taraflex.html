Heritage & Technology

OLYMPIC HERITAGE.
PROVEN EVERYWHERE.

Taraflex® has been engineered by Gerflor since 1947 — over seven decades of continuous innovation in indoor sports flooring. It is the most widely specified sports surface in the world, trusted in over 100 countries from school halls to Olympic arenas.

Selection for 10 consecutive Olympic Games — from Montréal 1976 to London 2012 — across badminton, volleyball, handball, table tennis and sitting volleyball is the ultimate proof of performance. Every Taraflex® surface passes the scrutiny of the world's best athletes and the strictest international federations.

At the heart of every Taraflex® product is Protecsol® triple-action anti-slip technology — a permanent surface treatment delivering consistent grip in every direction without ever compromising fluid movement. Combined with a complete P1, P2 and P3 shock absorption range, Taraflex® offers the right protection level for every sport, every user, and every budget.

As your authorized distributor in Malaysia, Rentak Hiasan Setia provides full supply, installation, and project management for every product in the Taraflex® range.
