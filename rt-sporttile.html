Technology

NBA-INSPIRED
COURT TECHNOLOGY

The design inspiration for RT SportTile comes directly from NBA basketball court structure. Each tile features 50 precision elastic cushions that deliver exceptional ball bounce and anti-slip performance that professional athletes demand.

The backside cross-rib structure provides stronger bearing capacity, avoids deformation, and ensures a better buffering effect under high-intensity play. Combined with our best elastic material innovation — preserving the elasticity of rubber with the strength of PP — the result is super flexible, non-deforming performance.

The new anti-arch design with elastic cushion and 2mm elastic gap prevents bulging and cracking in outdoor environments — critical for Malaysia's heat and humidity.

Our SG Safety Guard system is added for anti-theft and anti-wind security, using high-tech aviation material. Especially suited for harsh outdoor environments, easy to install and hidden within the tile structure.

Care Guide

HOW TO MAINTAIN
YOUR RT SPORTTILE

Soil, mud, fallen debris, moss and chemicals can all affect outdoor courts over time. Follow these maintenance recommendations to keep your RT SportTile court in peak condition.

💨
Basic Surface Clean
Use a leaf blower with tiles on the floor to remove light debris from the surface. PPE: gloves, goggles, auditory protection.
💧
Basic Tile Clean
Use a high-pressure cleaner at max 82 bars (1,200 psi) with water only. Removes debris from the interstices of tiles while tiles remain on ground.
🔧
Thorough Deep Clean
Lift all tiles. High-pressure wash at 82 bars through the tiles, then clean the substrate below. Removes vegetation and debris from interstices and base.
